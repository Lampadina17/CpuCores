Data & Transparency

Privacy Policy

CPU Cores is built with privacy at its core. Your device data belongs to you alone.

100% On-Device

Your data never leaves your device

CPU Cores does not send any data externally. The only metrics the app reads are displayed directly inside the app interface and never leave your device under any circumstances.

Zero External Connections

No analytics, no crash trackers, no telemetry, and no network calls to external servers.

Local-Only System Readings

CPU usage, RAM allocation, disk capacity, and hardware details are queried via local system APIs and rendered on-screen in real time.

No Accounts or Personal Info

We do not collect names, emails, identifiers, device IDs, or any identifiable personal data.
